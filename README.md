## 👋 Introduction to Revsha

**Revsha** is a modern Social Connection Platform designed to transform the way people discover one another and connect through meaningful in-person interactions.

Founded by **Erfan Abouei**, Revsha focuses on solving one of the challenges of modern social life: finding purposeful social connections and suitable places to meet in person.

We are not building just another social network or messaging tool; we are developing a platform that connects people with one another and with suitable places for in-person meetings through a simple, intuitive, and purposeful experience.

By connecting people and places, Revsha aims to make social discovery more accessible, help individuals find opportunities for meaningful interactions, and encourage connections that extend beyond the digital world.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

To empower people with a platform that simplifies social discovery, facilitates meaningful connections, and helps individuals find suitable places for in-person interactions.

We believe technology should bring people closer together while making it easier to turn digital discovery into genuine human experiences.

---

## 💡 Core Values & Focus Areas

At Revsha, our product philosophy is driven by five key pillars:

### 1. **Meaningful Social Connections**

We design the experience around purposeful interactions, helping people discover social connections that go beyond superficial engagement.

### 2. **Real-World Interaction**

Revsha connects digital discovery with in-person experiences, helping people move beyond online interactions and create opportunities to meet in real life.

### 3. **Simple & Human-Centered Design**

We believe social technology should be intuitive and accessible. Revsha aims to make discovering people and suitable meeting places simple and effortless.

### 4. **Trust & Safety**

Our product approach considers user privacy, trust, and responsible social interactions to support a safer and more reliable connection experience.

### 5. **Scalable Social Infrastructure**

Revsha is being designed around a maintainable and extensible product foundation that can evolve alongside user needs and future platform capabilities.
