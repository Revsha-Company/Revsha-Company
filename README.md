## 👋 Introduction to Revsha

**Revsha** is a social connection platform designed to help people build meaningful relationships and discover suitable places for in-person interactions.

Founded by **Erfan Abouei**, Revsha explores a simple but important idea: meaningful social connections should be easier to discover, establish, and experience in the real world.

We believe that technology should do more than keep people connected through screens. It should help turn digital discovery into real-world interactions by connecting people with one another and with places suited to meeting in person.

Revsha is being developed around purposeful social connections, bringing together people and places through an experience designed to make real-world interaction more accessible and intentional.

We are not simply building another social network. We are exploring a more purposeful approach to social discovery—one that places meaningful human interaction at the center of the experience.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make meaningful social connections more accessible by helping people discover one another and find suitable places to meet in person.

We believe technology should create opportunities for genuine human interaction, helping people move beyond digital engagement and toward meaningful experiences in the real world.

---

## 💡 Core Values & Focus Areas

At Revsha, our product philosophy is built around five key principles:

### 1. **Purposeful Social Connections**

We focus on helping people discover connections with intention, emphasizing meaningful interactions over superficial engagement.

### 2. **Real-World Interaction**

We believe the value of social technology extends beyond the screen. Revsha aims to bridge digital discovery and in-person experiences.

### 3. **People & Places**

We bring people and suitable meeting places into the same discovery experience, helping make real-world social interactions easier to pursue.

### 4. **Trust & Safety**

We recognize that meaningful social connections require trust, privacy, and responsible product design. These considerations are fundamental to how Revsha approaches its product experience.

### 5. **Simple & Human-Centered Design**

We aim to make social discovery intuitive and accessible, reducing unnecessary friction between discovering a potential connection and meeting in the real world.

## 🌍 What We Are Building

Revsha's product direction centers on purposeful social discovery and real-world connections.

Our vision includes:

- **People Discovery** — Helping individuals discover potential social connections.
- **Purposeful Connections** — Supporting interactions designed around meaningful social engagement.
- **Place Discovery** — Helping people identify suitable places for in-person meetings.
- **In-Person Experiences** — Connecting digital discovery with opportunities for real-world interaction.
- **Trust-Aware Experiences** — Considering privacy, safety, and user confidence throughout the product journey.
- **Human-Centered Technology** — Building a focused, intuitive experience around real human needs.

These areas describe our product direction, not a claim that every capability is already implemented or publicly available.

## 🌐 Our Vision

Founded by **Erfan Abouei**, Revsha represents a vision for a more purposeful way to connect people.

Social platforms have made it easier than ever to communicate digitally, yet discovering meaningful connections and turning them into real-world interactions can remain difficult.
